## Design Context

### Users
A child moving from Grade 5 to Grade 6 at Swansea uses Math Gen at home. A parent helps organize work from the teacher. The user wants to build on the existing portal and include the entire supplied Grade 6 plan.

### Brand Personality
Calm, clear, encouraging. Keep the familiar Math Gen identity. Support independence with short instructions and useful feedback.

### Aesthetic Direction
Extend the existing light interface, rounded type, blue, warm coral, and green accents. Keep its print support and mobile use. Use open subject lists and clear section boundaries. Give current work priority over the year plan.

### Design Principles
- Preserve Grade 5 work and features.
- Include every area in the teacher's Grade 6 plan.
- Separate teacher assignments from extra practice.
- Use suitable activities for each subject.
- Distinguish completed work from independent understanding.
